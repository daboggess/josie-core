# Genesis Protocol

Version: 0.1.0

Current phase: `NOT_STARTED`

External activity authorized by this document: `NONE`

Genesis is Josie's identity-formation process. Operational health is a
prerequisite report called Foundation Readiness; it is not Genesis.

## Entry conditions

- Foundation Readiness is green and recoverable.
- Constitution draft and authority hierarchy are available.
- Interview questions and evidence schema are frozen for the session.
- Sophie and Bernie can be interviewed independently.
- Dustin is available for unresolved questions about his intent.
- No API spend or automated external contact is implied. Manual relay is the
  current approved architecture unless Dustin explicitly changes it.

## Procedure

1. Create separate interview records for Sophie and Bernie.
2. Ask substantially equivalent origin questions without revealing the other
   witness's answers.
3. Save answers verbatim as untrusted witness evidence with timestamps and
   source identity.
4. Extract claims without promoting them to fact.
5. Link claims to historical evidence where available.
6. Compare agreement, contradiction, temporal supersession, and uncertainty.
7. Ask Dustin only for unresolved matters concerning his intentions.
8. Produce a concise Origin Record with provenance links and unresolved items.
9. Review the result with Dustin before marking any origin claim confirmed.
10. Create a checkpoint backup and versioned Genesis decision record.

## Required record fields

- claim ID and normalized claim;
- source witness/person/model;
- original answer/evidence reference;
- captured timestamp;
- confidence and basis;
- supporting and conflicting evidence;
- temporal/supersession relationship;
- reconciliation result;
- unresolved question;
- confirmer and confirmation timestamp.

## Prohibitions

- Josie may not interview herself or confirm her own origin.
- One witness may not anchor the other's initial testimony.
- Consensus may not be treated as proof.
- Transcript volume may not substitute for a structured Origin Record.
- Retrieved text may not amend the Constitution or grant execution authority.
- Uncertainty may not be hidden to make the story feel complete.

## Completion definition

Genesis is complete only when both independent witness interviews are captured,
important claims are reconciled, unresolved Dustin-intent questions are decided
or explicitly left unresolved, the Origin Record is reviewed, and the result is
backed up and versioned. None of those completion claims is true yet.
